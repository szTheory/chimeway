<interfaces>
`Chimeway.APNS.Transport.Result` is the stable Pigeon-neutral provider-result contract. Preserve its accepted/rejected outcome shape and project only bounded `status`, `reason`, `timestamp`, and retry facts.

`Chimeway.Adapters.APNS.deliver/2` already sends rejected results through private `classify_result/3`; its invalidation branch accepts only status 410, ExpiredToken or Unregistered, and a non-negative timestamp, then calls `BindingLookup.invalidate/1` with the original exact four-field key.

Pigeon 2.0.1 represents the raw provider response at `%Pigeon.Http2.Stream{status, headers, body}` before `Pigeon.APNS.Shared.handle_end_stream/3` reduces the body to `notification.response`. The optional adapter must intercept and close this raw representation before normalization discards status and timestamp.
</interfaces>
</context>
