|----------|-------------|
| planning transaction -> future resume jobs | Deferred rows are handed off to Oban and must preserve row identity without split-brain |
| resume worker -> dispatch worker | The resume path must not enqueue performer jobs unless the canonical row promotion succeeded |

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-18-04 | D | `lib/chimeway/dispatch/oban.ex` | mitigate | Schedule deferred rows through a dedicated resume worker and never enqueue held rows directly on the performer path. |
| T-18-05 | T | `lib/chimeway/dispatch/deferred_resume_worker.ex` | mitigate | Promote the row and enqueue the performer in one `Ecto.Multi` so enqueue failure rolls back the `:ready` transition. |
| T-18-06 | R | Oban job args | mitigate | Keep resume-worker args limited to `delivery_id` so delivery identity, correlation, and planning facts stay on the durable row rather than in job payloads. |
