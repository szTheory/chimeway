<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| scheduler/resume callers -> delivery row transition helpers | Async callers must not be able to resume rows that are no longer pending, deferred, and due |
| delivery row metadata -> operator trace surfaces | Resume audit facts are exposed later and must remain sanitized string-keyed state only |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-18-01 | T | `lib/chimeway/deliveries.ex` | mitigate | Require a conditional row update on `status`, `orchestration_state`, and `next_eligible_at` so repeated resume attempts cannot mutate or win after cancellation/supersession. |
| T-18-02 | R | `lib/chimeway/deliveries.ex` | mitigate | Persist `resume_source`, `resume_scheduled_at`, and `resumed_at` on the canonical row so later traces can show who resumed the delivery and when. |
| T-18-03 | I | delivery metadata | mitigate | Limit metadata additions to scheduler timestamps and source labels; do not store payload, provider, or recipient-sensitive fields. |
</threat_model>
