the 1.17 floor leg run AND gate on push (QUAL-05). Both are `ci.yml`-only edits layered on the
version-file conversion from plan 91-01, so this runs in wave 2.

be genuinely exercised and enforced on push, not left as a silent/advisory leg (the vacuous-pass
footgun called out in project memory).

Output: top-level `permissions: contents: read` + per-job `actions: read` on the 15 obs-summary jobs;
a new `run_floor` output on `resolve_tiers`; the floor `if:` broadened to push+nightly; `ci-gate`
extended to require and aggregate `TEST_FLOOR_1_17`. `release.yml`, `nightly-gate`, `pr-gate`, and
`aggregate-gate.sh` are untouched.
</objective>
