<interfaces>
- `Chimeway.Privacy.redact/1` remains a total term-to-term function: maps retain allowed keys in their original representation, lists retain sequence, keyword-shaped lists retain duplicate allowed pairs and order, and scalar values pass through.
- Explicit temporal scalar structs `%Date{}`, `%Time{}`, `%NaiveDateTime{}`, and `%DateTime{}` retain their struct representation because SafeEvidence validators and trace ordering consume them as scalar facts. Every other struct is converted with `Map.from_struct/1` and recursively filtered as an ordinary map; no caller-controlled key is atomized.
- `Chimeway.SafeEvidence.proof/1` remains the proof/diagnostic redaction seam and must inherit the corrected struct behavior without a separate policy.
- `Chimeway.SafeEvidence.trace/1` retains its established top-level literal keys. A valid `last_attempt` is projected with `trace_attempt/1`; nil or malformed values become nil.
- A valid timeline entry has exactly `:at`, `:event`, and `:detail`. `:at` is a DateTime, `:event` is one of the lifecycle events already emitted by `Chimeway.Traces`, and `:detail` is rebuilt with `timeline_detail/1` or defaults to `%{}`.
- The closed timeline event vocabulary is `:event_created`, `:notification_created`, `:delivery_planned`, `:deferred`, `:resumed`, `:recovered`, `:suppressed`, `:cancelled`, `:digested`, `:digest_skipped`, `:emitted_immediately`, `:digest_emitted`, `:attempt_recorded`, `:webhook_received`, `:workflow_progressed`, `:workflow_waiting`, `:workflow_stopped`, and `:workflow_completed`.
- A malformed timeline term, non-DateTime `:at`, unknown/non-atom event, or non-map detail is omitted rather than forwarded. Valid entries retain their input order; `Traces` remains responsible for chronological construction.
- `trace_attempt/1` retains only its existing closed keys and validators: lifecycle ID, outcome, inserted timestamp, attempt number, error class, and opaque provider message reference. Provider response/body values are never part of this map.
</interfaces>
