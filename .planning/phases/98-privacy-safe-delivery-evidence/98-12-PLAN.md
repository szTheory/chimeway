<interfaces>
- `SafeEvidence.opaque_ref(:recipient, value)` is the documented host-supplied `cw_...` validator: 4..160 bytes, lowercase `cw_` prefix, then lowercase ASCII alphanumeric with internal underscore or hyphen.
- `SafeEvidence.recipient_reference/1` is called by both `Trigger.normalize_recipients/1` and `Workflows.find_runs_waiting_for_signal/3`; it returns `{:ok, value}` only for an exact accepted reference and `{:error, :unsafe_evidence}` for all other terms.
- The intentional compatibility form is exactly `user:` followed by one canonical lowercase UUID (`8-4-4-4-12` lowercase hexadecimal groups); values such as `user:alex-smith`, `user:alice`, `user:alex@example.test`, uppercase UUID spellings, and malformed UUIDs are rejected.
- `Trigger.normalize_recipients/1` canonicalizes recipient maps before `notifications_attrs/6`; its accepted `:recipient_ref` output is the value written to `chimeway_notifications.recipient_identity`.
- `Workflows.find_runs_waiting_for_signal/3` validates `Signal.actor_id` with the same function before matching `Notification.recipient_identity`; an unsafe actor produces no matched runs.
</interfaces>
</context>
